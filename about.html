About Your Playmate Sock Dolls

Your Playmate Sock Dolls, LLC creates handcrafted sock dolls designed to bring comfort, joy, and emotional support to seriously ill children.

Each doll is soft, safe, washable, and made with care to brighten the days of young patients.

Our mission is simple: to bring warmth, happiness, and hope to children who need it most.

Our Story

Founded by Susan York and Tiffany Brown, Your Playmate Sock Dolls began as a small passion project supporting children’s hospitals. What started with handmade dolls crafted at home quickly grew into a meaningful partnership with care centers across the country.

As demand increased, Susan and Tiffany managed orders using a mix of Microsoft 365 tools, spreadsheets, and even handwritten notes. With the business expanding and more hospitals relying on their products, they knew it was time to move toward a modern, accessible ecommerce system.

Today, Your Playmate Sock Dolls continues to grow while staying true to its purpose: bringing comfort to children through thoughtful, handcrafted creations.
